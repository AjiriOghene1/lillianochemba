Rooted in Faith, Built for People.

Lillian Chidinma Ochemba is a pastor, author, certified holistic life coach, entrepreneur, speaker, community builder and advocate for women, families and purposeful living.

She is passionate about helping people discover who they are, grow in their relationship with God, develop healthy relationships, lead with wisdom and live lives of meaningful impact.

Lillian combines her faith, leadership experience, professional training and passion for people development to create spaces where people can learn, grow, heal, lead and become.

She is particularly passionate about women, especially women in ministry, marriage and leadership, and has dedicated significant portions of her work to helping women become more confident, emotionally healthy, spiritually grounded and purpose-driven.

She is the founder of Oneness.ng, the umbrella platform for her work in outreach, human development, entrepreneurship and spiritual growth.

Lillian is married to Reverend Chima Ochemba and is a mother, pastor and mentor to many.

Her Story.

Lillian's story is one of faith, growth, service and transformation.

Her journey has been shaped by a deep desire to see people become all that God created them to be. Over the years, this passion has taken her through ministry, leadership, entrepreneurship, coaching, writing, community building and social impact.

As she grew in her own understanding of identity, purpose, marriage, leadership and spiritual maturity, she became increasingly passionate about helping others navigate these same areas.

Her work today is therefore not simply built around what she has learned, but around what she has lived, studied, taught and continues to discover.

Through different seasons, Lillian has continued to evolve while remaining anchored in one central conviction:

People flourish when they understand who they are, understand whose they are, and are equipped to live intentionally.

Her story continues to unfold through the lives she influences, the women she mentors, the families she strengthens, the communities she builds and the ideas she brings to life.

Oneness Outreach.

Oneness Outreach is how Oneness.ng goes into communities directly. It is not one fixed programme: it takes the shape of whatever is needed in a place and a season.

“Wherever the need is, that’s where we go.”

The outreach is deliberately not streamlined. In one season it looks like seedlings and sacks of soil. In another it looks like sanitary pads and a conversation with a room of schoolgirls about their own worth. In another it looks like food items and clothing, or digging toilets for a community that has none.

What stays the same is why: to put a smile on someone’s face, to give people hope, and to introduce them to Jesus.

What We Do.

Agricultural Outreach.

Teaching women to grow their own food in sacks, bags and buckets, which works even without land of their own, and buying the seedlings to start them off. For the families who took it up, it changed what that season looked like.

Girls’ Outreach.

Run several times across different schools: sanitary pads and hygiene, self-confidence, and teaching girls to take care of and protect their bodies. Practical things, handed over alongside the conversation that should come with them.

School Outreach.

Working with children and students on a skill they can carry with them: something in the hand as well as something in the head.

Medical Outreach.

Taking basic health care to communities that have little access to it: checks, treatment and the advice that should come with them, given where people already are rather than somewhere they have to travel to reach.

Gospel Outreach.

Taking the message out directly, in the open and in conversation. It runs through everything else the outreach does, and sometimes it is the whole of it.

Church and Community Projects.

Food items and clothing for families, and practical work in the communities themselves, including digging toilets where there are none.

How It Works.

There is no fixed calendar and no single template. Each outreach starts with what a particular community actually needs, and is put together around that, which is why one looks nothing like the next.

Every outreach is carried out under Oneness.ng, the umbrella platform for Lillian’s work in outreach, human development, entrepreneurship and spiritual growth.

Wise Wives.

A community for wives who want stronger, healthier and more intentional marriages, built on faith, and on the practical wisdom of walking it out every day.

Wise Wives began in 2025, just after Lillian released The Wise Wife’s Blueprint. The book set out what a wise wife looks like; Wise Wives is where wives keep learning it, together, long after the last page.

It is not a place for perfect marriages. It is a place for wives who want theirs to grow, who are willing to learn, to be honest about where things are hard, and to keep choosing wisdom over reaction.

What Wise Wives Is About.

Wisdom for the Home.

Practical, faith-rooted teaching on the everyday work of marriage: how a wise wife thinks, speaks, chooses and builds.

Communication and Conflict.

Learning to say what needs saying, to listen, and to handle disagreement in a way that draws a couple closer instead of pushing them apart.

Prayer.

Putting prayer at the centre of the marriage, for your husband, your home and yourself, rather than keeping it for emergencies.

Community.

Wives walking the same road, encouraging one another and learning from each other’s seasons, so no one has to work it out alone.

Lady Vessels.

A safe and empowering community for women in ministry, and one of the initiatives carried out under Oneness.ng.

Women in ministry spend much of their lives pouring into others: leading, serving, counselling, praying, holding things together. Very few spaces exist where they can be the ones who are poured into.

Lady Vessels is that space: a community where women serving in ministry can be honest about the realities of the calling, be refreshed in their own walk with God, and be strengthened alongside others who understand.

Lady Vessels also comes out of Lillian’s own beginning. When she first stepped into ministry, everything was new to her, and much of the time she felt completely alone in it. Many of her early lessons were learned the hard way.

So Lady Vessels is also for women who are just starting out: a place to find their feet quickly, learn from those who have walked the road before them, and avoid the mistakes Lillian made at her own starting point.

What Lady Vessels Is About.

A Safe Place.

Somewhere to speak freely about the weight of ministry, its pressures, loneliness and questions, without having to perform or be the strong one in the room.

Spiritual Refreshing.

Time to tend your own relationship with God, not just the relationships of the people you serve, so that what you give others flows from a full place.

Strength for the Work.

Encouragement and practical wisdom for the unique realities women face in ministry, leadership and service: balancing the calling with family, health and everything else life asks.

Sisterhood.

Women walking the same road, praying for one another and sharing what they have learned, so that no one has to carry the calling alone.

A Head Start for Those Just Beginning.

For women who are new to ministry: guidance from those further along the road, so they can grow into the calling faster and avoid the mistakes that are so easy to make at the start.

Who It’s For.

Women serving in ministry in any capacity: pastors, ministers’ wives, worship and church leaders, women running ministries of their own, those who are new to ministry and still finding their feet, and those who sense a call and are preparing to answer it.

Believers Recharge Retreat.

At the beginning of each year, Oneness.ng gathers believers to prepare for the year ahead: to recharge, and to seek God’s counsel on how the year should go.

Most people step into a new year still carrying the weight of the last one. The Believers Recharge Retreat exists for that reason: to start the year filled rather than depleted, and with a sense of direction rather than a list of resolutions.

It began in 2025, built around exactly that purpose.

Ministry, work, family and service all draw from the same well, and the people who carry others rarely stop long enough to be carried themselves. For the length of the retreat, nothing is asked of you. You come, you are present, and you receive.

What to Expect.

Worship and the Word.

Unhurried sessions with room to linger. No tight programme to keep to, no rushing from one thing to the next: time to actually sit in what is being taught and let it settle.

Teaching That Goes Deeper.

Sessions built for people who already know the basics and are hungry for more: spiritual maturity, discipline, purpose and the practical shape of walking with God over the long term.

Rest That Is Actually Rest.

Space to breathe, think and be quiet. Rest is not an interval between sessions here; it is part of what the retreat is for.

Fellowship.

Time with people walking the same road. Some of what the retreat gives happens in the sessions; a good deal of it happens in the conversations around them.

Seeking Direction for the Year.

Time set aside to pray over what is coming and listen for His direction, so the months ahead are entered deliberately, with a sense of where you are going and why.

Who It’s For.

Anyone who has been pouring out and needs to be poured into: those serving in ministry, leading at home or at work, or simply carrying more than they have said out loud. First-timers are as welcome as those who have been since the beginning.
